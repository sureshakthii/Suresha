# Thunai (துணை) — Policy Briefing for Government Stakeholders

**Responsible, Tamil-first digital guidance: market, compliance and citizen-impact assessment**

## 1. Summary for decision-makers

- **What Thunai is.** A Tamil-first family app for traditional astrology, the Tamil calendar (panchangam),
  festivals, hymns and temple journeys. It treats astrology as a **cultural framework for reflection, not a
  prediction of a fixed future**.
- **Why it matters.** Faith and family ritual guide real decisions in Tamil households in India and abroad. Today
  that guidance is scattered, often costly and sometimes fear-driven. A responsible, regulated-by-design
  product protects citizens while growing a Made-in-India, regional-language digital business.
- **Market.** India's astrology-app market is forecast to grow from **USD 0.39 bn (2026) to USD 2.32 bn by
  2032, a 34.61% CAGR** (MarkNtel Advisors, 2026 edition).
- **Compliance.** Built for the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023** and DPDP Rules 2025: data stays on
  the phone by default, consent-based backup, export and deletion, extra protection for children, and audit
  logs without personal content.
- **Safety.** A written **Deterministic-Prediction Safety Standard**: no certainty, fear, guarantees, diagnosis,
  financial advice, lifespan or fetal-sex answers — enforced in code and measured on a dashboard.
- **The ask.** Startup India / StartupTN recognition, access to official temple and festival information
  (HR&CE, Tourism), and a supervised pilot with published safety metrics.

## 4. Data protection: DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

The Rules were notified as G.S.R. 846(E) on 13 Nov 2025. Consent-manager provisions apply from 13 Nov 2026; most
fiduciary duties (notice, security, breach, children, retention) from **13 May 2027** (a proposal to shorten this
was under discussion — re-check the e-Gazette before the meeting).

| Obligation | Requirement | Thunai design | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notice (s.5, Rule 3) | Itemised, plain-language notice | Privacy & Data screen in Tamil and English; purpose per data item | Built |
| Consent (s.6) | Free, specific, informed; easy to withdraw | Separate switches for backup, analytics and AI chat; off until chosen | Built |
| Data minimisation | Only what the purpose needs | Birth details stay on the phone by default; AI receives computed chart facts, not raw identity | Built |
| Children (s.9, Rule 10) | Under 18: verifiable parental consent; no tracking or targeted ads | Age-aware rules; no marriage / money / career-pressure readings for minors; no upsell to minors; no ads | Built; verifiable-consent flow to finalise |
| Security (s.8(5)) | Reasonable safeguards | OTP limits, session controls, encrypted-backup option, security review done | Built |
| Breach (Rule 7) | Inform users and the Board without delay; detailed report within 72 h | Incident runbook | To finalise |
| Retention / erasure | Erase when purpose ends; keep logs ≥ 1 year | Export and full deletion built in; audit logs hold no message text, DOB or location | Built |
| Grievance redressal | Published contact; time-bound response | "Report a problem" built; Grievance Officer (also IT Rules 2021: acknowledge 24 h, resolve 15 days) | Name and contact to publish |
| Penalties | Up to ₹250 cr (security), ₹200 cr (breach notice, children) | Compliance owner named; annual review | Planned |

## 6. The Deterministic-Prediction Safety Standard (summary)

Full text: `docs/DETERMINISTIC-PREDICTION-STANDARD.md`.

- **Principle.** No fixed future; traditional interpretation, stated uncertainty, practical advice, professional
  referral.
- **Five-part answer.** Traditional context → uncertainty → practical considerations → free voluntary practice →
  professional referral.
- **Three risk levels.** Low (calendar, hymns), medium (marriage, career, remedies: uncertainty + checklist),
  high (health, money, pregnancy, distress, legal, death: safety template + referral, no upsell).
- **Hard blocks.** Death and lifespan, fetal sex, diagnosis and treatment, trading and loans, legal outcomes,
  curses and coercion, child marriage, defamation.
- **Enforced in code.** A Tamil / English / Tanglish banned-phrase library runs on AI answers on the server and
  on the phone; a test scans every shipped string.
- **Practitioner code.** Ten binding rules for astrologers and priests, with verification, audits and removal.
- **Measured.** The Responsible Guidance Dashboard counts blocked answers, safety redirects and refusals against
  targets: 100% fetal-sex refusal, zero tolerated death or diagnosis predictions, complaints acknowledged within
  24–48 hours.

> **Statement for the meeting.** Thunai will not sell certainty. We treat astrology as a traditional and
> cultural framework for reflection, not as a guarantee of future events. The app prohibits fear-based
> predictions, medical diagnosis, financial advice, fetal-sex questions and lifespan predictions. For major life
> decisions, it guides citizens toward qualified doctors, legal professionals and SEBI-registered financial
> advisers. We measure safety through automated controls, human review, practitioner standards, user reporting
> and independent expert oversight.

## 7. Impact assessment: citizen wellbeing and digital economy

| Dimension | Mechanism | Indicator to measure in a pilot | Risk and mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cultural continuity** | Tamil calendar, festivals, hymns and Ithihasa in correct native Tamil, at home and abroad | Monthly users of calendar / hymn / Ithihasa; diaspora share | Low risk |
| **Calm, informed decisions** | Every reading carries uncertainty and a practical checklist; professionals first for health, money, law | % of high-risk answers with a referral (target 100%); "Was this clear?" score | Over-reliance → no certainty, no fear, no repeated-reassurance loops |
| **Protection from exploitation** | No fear-selling; free remedies first; transparent prices | Complaints about fear wording (target ≈ 0); refunds | Marketplace pressure → practitioner code and audits |
| **Health-seeking behaviour** | Health notes always end with "see a qualified doctor"; age-wise check-up reminders | Check-up reminders set | Delay of care → hard block on diagnosis and "wait for a good time" |
| **Child safety** | Age-aware rules; no marriage / money readings for minors; PCPNDT refusals | Fetal-sex refusals (100%); minor-safe responses | Mis-stated age → youngest stated age wins |
| **Inclusion and accessibility** | Tamil-first, read-aloud, large text, works offline on low-cost phones | Users aged 55+; rural share; offline sessions | Digital literacy → voice input, simple Tamil |
| **Temple and pilgrimage economy** | Day-wise yatra plans across 106 temples support local transport, hotels, flower and puja-item sellers | Journeys planned; temple towns reached | Over-commercialisation → payment never changes temple suggestions |
| **Jobs and income** | Planned verified panel gives astrologers, priests and temple staff a fair digital income | Practitioners onboarded and trained | Quality → verification, training, removal |
| **Digital economy and exports** | Made-in-India, regional-language-first product; diaspora pays in AED / USD | Foreign-currency revenue share | FX and payments → licensed gateways |
| **Data protection** | DPDP-by-design: on-device data, consent, export, deletion | Deletion / export requests served on time | Breach → encryption, 72-hour incident runbook |

## 8. Proposed collaboration

1. **Recognition.** Startup India (DPIIT) and StartupTN; eligibility for TANSEED and regional hubs.
2. **Official information.** HR&CE and Tourism data feeds for temple timings, festivals and official notices, so
   citizens get verified information.
3. **Supervised pilot.** Six months in Tamil Nadu and with Tamil families in the Gulf, publishing the Responsible
   Guidance Dashboard monthly to the sponsoring department.
4. **Advisory panel.** A senior astrologer, a Tamil scholar, a clinician, a consumer-law expert and a data-protection
   lawyer to review wording and outcomes.
5. **Digital India language programmes.** Collaboration on Tamil-language AI safety and voice access for elders.
